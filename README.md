LEONIDA NIGHTS — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, top-down 2D in vanilla JS + Canvas. Explore the night city of Leonida on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police under a wanted level, and play three story missions with dialogue: a midnight package run, a police dragnet escape, and the Bank of Leonida heist. Traffic and pedestrians, combat (punch and pistol), pickups, a HUD with a minimap, start screen and localStorage saves, pause and restart, and synthesised sound.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Keyboard: WASD to walk/drive, Shift to run, E to enter/steal a car, click to shoot, F to punch, Space to brake, P to pause, M to mute. Mouse works for all menus.
